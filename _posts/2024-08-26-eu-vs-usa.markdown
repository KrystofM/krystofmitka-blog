Classic question: What is the opposite of hate?

Had a discussion about love with my friend and started thinking what is the relation between hate and love.

I was trying to explain that indeed hate and love might be perpendicular to each other in some dimensions of the space such as "goodness of the feeling" or "who am I more likely to kill". But the question really hints at the fact that having a strong good feeling towards somebody can be really quickly be switched to having a really bad feeling about somebody. I believe this can be illustrated by the act of cheating. Somehow the representation .

In essence the two feelings should be very close in the dimension of the space that measures the "strength of a feeling" or something along the lines. It is hard for me to describe the exact feature where love and hate are close to each other in space and "indifference" is perpendicular, but I believe there is one.

Idea: Can we settle this discussion using the embedding space of LLMs? Surely LLMs must have a learned good representation of our feelings after reading terabytes of the internet trying to predict what Romeo and Julia will say to each one token at a time. What are the fetures where

Maybe this is a new field? Understanding our brains using the brains of AI? If this picks up lets call it
