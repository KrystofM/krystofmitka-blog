Questions:

What is society converging to?

Society = cluster of human brains, directional graph where the weight of each node is defined by the amount of data transfer. Data transfer between the nodes of the graph (people) in its purest form is defined using an encoding of language, while other forms exist such as body language, appearance and actions. People are the most susceptable to transferring information using language ~ we form priors using language and then over time update them based on the actions of the other people. This is because language likely emerged as a form of exchanging information about danger in the environment people live in, meaning brains assign high trust prior to communication using language. (maybe support more the idea that language is central information exchange between human brains)
